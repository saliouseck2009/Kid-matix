# Privacy policy — Kid Matix

[Français](privacy-policy.html) · **English** · [Español](privacy-policy-es.html)

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Kid Matix is an app that helps children learn their multiplication tables by
playing. It was made for children: it runs entirely on the phone or the
tablet, with no account and no connection to the Internet.

## In short

- No account, no email address, no password.
- No data leaves the device: the app never connects to the Internet.
- No advertising, no in-app purchase, no link to the outside.
- No analytics tool, no tracker, no third-party service.
- The game data is erased by deleting the player in the app or by
  uninstalling the app.

## The data stored on the device

Several children can play on the same device, each with their own profile
(10 profiles at most). For each profile, the app stores on the device only:

- the nickname chosen by the child (2 to 12 characters), the avatar and the
  colour chosen among those of the app;
- the progress: answers given, answer times, mastery of each multiplication,
  stars, crowns, experience points (XP), level, streak of days played,
  badges, records;
- the mascot: its name and the accessories it wears;
- the settings of the player: sounds, vibrations, timer, goal of the day,
  "Unlock everything", reduced animations.

The app also remembers the last player chosen and the last choices made on
the "Practice" screen, to offer them again the next time it opens.

The nickname is the only information entered by the child. We advise using a
nickname rather than the real first and last name of the child.

This data serves the working of the game only: showing the progress,
choosing the questions to review, awarding the rewards.

## What the app does not do

- It asks for one permission only, and only if the daily reminder is turned
  on in the settings: showing notifications. The reminder is scheduled on
  the phone, without a connection. It asks for neither Internet, nor camera,
  nor microphone, nor location, nor contacts, nor photos.
- It sends no data, neither to us nor to anyone. We have access to no
  information about the players.
- It shows no advertising and offers no purchase.
- It uses no analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tool that talks to a
  server.
- It does not let the players exchange anything with each other, nor publish
  anything.

The sounds and the vibrations of the game are produced by the device, from
files included in the app.

## How long the data is kept, and how to erase it

The data stays on the device as long as the profile exists. To erase it:

- **Delete a player**: Profile tab, then Settings, section "This player",
  "Delete this player". To confirm, the nickname of the player has to be
  written again. All the data of this player is erased.
- **Start over**: in the same place, "Reset my progress" erases the progress
  of the player and keeps their profile, after the same confirmation.
- **Erase everything**: uninstalling the app deletes all the data of all the
  players.

As nothing is sent to a server, erased data cannot be recovered, and
changing device starts from zero, except with a backup file (see below).

## Backup to a file

In Settings, section "Backup", an adult can export a profile to a file
(nickname, avatar, settings, progress and mascot of this player) and share
it themselves with the tools of the phone, for example to import it on
another device; a parental check protects the export and the import. The app
sends nothing: the file goes only where the user chooses to send it.

In class mode, the class files (the pupils' nicknames and their results)
leave the device only when the teacher shares them from the teacher area,
which is protected by their code.

The automatic backup of the system (Google account or iCloud) is turned off
for Kid Matix: the data of the game is not copied there.

## Children

The app is meant for children from 6 years old. It collects no personal data
and transmits nothing: it therefore follows the rules of Google Play for the
apps made for families, those of the Kids category of the App Store, the
GDPR and the United States COPPA law.

## Changes

A future version could offer an optional online mode (leaderboards, backup
to an account). In that case, this policy will be updated before its
release, and the consent of a parent will be asked where the law requires
it. As long as this page does not announce it, the app stays entirely
offline.
